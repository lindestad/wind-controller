# Third-party material

Zephyr and its dependencies are fetched separately at pinned revisions. Each retains
its upstream license; they are not vendored here. KiCad libraries are required for
standard 3D STEP models.

The project-local `hardware/libraries/Wind.pretty` footprints and symbol library include
adaptations of KiCad library material. That material remains under KiCad's **CC BY-SA
4.0 with the library exception**, including the exception for designs using the libraries.
See https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/ and the original KiCad libraries at
https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries. Project-specific dimensioned visual models are
representative aids, not manufacturer-certified CAD.

Supplier part numbers and manufacturer names identify compatible components; they do
not imply endorsement. Third-party datasheets, proprietary SDK binaries and account/order
screenshots are not redistributed. Python packages listed in requirements are installed
separately and retain their own licenses.
